Radhiant is a small group of companies. This entry describes what each one does. It is a working description for staff. It does not set out legal ownership or signing authority; those come from the company records.

## The entities

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| Radhiant Group | Group level: strategy and matters that cut across entities. |
| Radhiant Diagnostic Imaging South Africa (RDISA) | The clinical service: mobile mammography. HPCSA and BHF registrations sit with RDISA. |
| Radhiant Technologies | The asset and technology vehicle: trucks, mammography machines and equipment. |
| Radhiant Properties | Property holding. |
| Radhiant Foundation | A separate non-profit company. It provides the free mammograms under Mammogram Match. |
| Radhiant Consulting | Dormant. |
| Radhiant Investments | Dormant. |
| Radhiant Holdings UK plc | UK-registered entity. |

Every Group entity holds Level 1 B-BBEE.

## Which entity for which work

- Clinical, practice, radiology, patient and referrer matters: **RDISA**.
- Operational, vendor, equipment and employment matters: **Radhiant Technologies**.
- Group-level and cross-entity matters: **Radhiant Group**.
- Property: **Radhiant Properties**.
- Donations and free screening: **Radhiant Foundation**.

Use the right entity's letterhead for outbound documents (see the Brand guide entry). If you are unsure which entity signs something, ask before it goes out.

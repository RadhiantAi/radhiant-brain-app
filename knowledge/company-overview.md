Radhiant Group is a South African healthcare company founded by Dr Anith Chacko, a specialist radiologist. The core business is **Radhiant Diagnostic Imaging South Africa (RDISA)**, the largest fully mobile national mammography operator.

Radhiant exists to increase access to breast screening for all women.

## What RDISA does

Seven mobile 3D mammography units work from five bases (Johannesburg, Bronkhorstspruit, Durban, East London and Cape Town). They go to workplaces, clinics and communities so that women can be screened where they are.

Our public promise is a report within 3 to 5 working days.

## The figures we use

- **25,000+ mammograms** done, as at 1 November 2026. This counts examinations, which is different from the number of individual women.
- **450+ suspicious findings** (BI-RADS 4 to 5) referred for biopsy, as at 1 November 2026. These are referrals. Never call them cancers detected or cancers diagnosed.
- **300+ locations** across all nine provinces, as at 1 November 2026.

Always give the date with these figures. They rise almost every month, so check for a newer figure before using them outside the company.

## Why it matters

About 2 million South African women have medical aid and about 8 million do not. Of the 2 million, about 400,000 have a mammogram each year. Of the 8 million, about 80,000 do (market context from Anith, 7 November 2026; estimated).

## How women reach us

1. Through their medical aid.
2. Through their employer, on a workplace screening day.
3. As a cash patient. The cash price of a screening mammogram is R1,200.
4. Through HerWay, our monthly subscription (see the Outreach and growth entry).
5. Through Mammogram Match (below).

## Mammogram Match

For every paid mammogram booked with RDISA, the Radhiant Foundation provides one free mammogram to a woman without medical aid. Mammogram Match is permanent. It began as a pilot in August 2026 and was made permanent at the end of October 2026.

## Purpose, vision and mission

From the RDISA Vision, mission and values document, Version 4.4 of 28 September 2026.

- **Purpose:** to save lives by bringing early breast cancer detection directly to women, with compassion, dignity and clinical excellence.
- **Vision:** a South Africa where no woman dies because access, fear, distance or delay stood in the way of early breast cancer detection.
- **Mission:** we deliver mobile mammography with clinical accuracy, reliable operations and care for each woman. Our growth aim is 50,000 women a year by 2028, while keeping the service safe and dependable.

We count completed safe breast-health journeys. A completed journey means a woman is on the right route, has understood her result and has accepted a next step or follow-up. A scan produced or a report sent does not, on its own, complete the journey.

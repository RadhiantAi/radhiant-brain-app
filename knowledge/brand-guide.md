## Colours by entity

Primary for all Radhiant design is **Tropical Night Blue `#2B2E4B`**, paired with the entity accent:

| Entity | Accent |
|---|---|
| Radhiant Group | Orange `#EF5727` |
| Radhiant Technologies | Baltic Teal `#229FA1` |
| RDISA | Verse Green `#1D8A43` |
| Radhiant Properties | Plum `#6B4F90` |
| Radhiant Consulting (dormant) | Red `#DB322A` |
| Radhiant Investments (dormant) | Goldenrod `#DEA92E` |

Group-wide or cross-entity work uses Tropical Night Blue with the Group orange. The RDISA-facing Admin Portal uses the Verse Green accent. This app is group-wide, so it uses orange.

## Letterheads

Every entity has a letterhead master. Formal outbound documents go on the right entity's letterhead: clinical and patient matters on RDISA, operational and vendor matters on Radhiant Technologies, group matters on Radhiant Group. Export finals to PDF so the Montserrat typography holds.

## Writing style

- UK and South African English: organisation, colour, recognise.
- Short paragraphs, plain words, absolute dates ("by Tuesday 14 July 2026", never "next week").
- No em dashes. No corporate buzzwords.
- We say **outreach**, never "sales". The team, the function and the job titles are all outreach.
- We describe Radhiant as increasing access to screening for all women.
- Third person for external and investor documents.
- Confidentiality tag on every document: Public, Internal or Confidential.

If you know the rhythm, you know where decisions get made. For times, attendance and links, the calendar invitation is always right.

## Weekly

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday 10:00 to 11:30 SAST | **L10** (the management meeting): scorecard, quarterly rocks, to-dos, and IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve) | Anith chairs, with the five department heads |
| Friday 08:15 SAST | **Trust Circle**, followed by the all-hands group hug | The group hug is for everyone |
| Friday | **Weekly manager reports** are filed | The five department heads |
| Friday | **Weekly update** from Anith | The five department heads |
| Daily | **Daily huddle** | Teams |

The L10 runs off the Company Dashboard in the Admin Portal. A pre-read goes out before each L10.

## Meetings that have ended

- The Wednesday Leadership Meeting ended on 11 September 2026.

## Manager reports

Each department head files a weekly report on Friday. Anith replies with feedback, and the manager answers in writing by close of business the next day.

## Quarterly

Quarterly rocks are set and reviewed each quarter. Q4 2026 is the current quarter.

## Ground rules

- Every scorecard metric has one owner. Reds get discussed, never hidden.
- To-dos leave a meeting with an owner and an absolute date.

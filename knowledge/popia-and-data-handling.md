Radhiant handles two kinds of sensitive information: **patient data** (special personal information under POPIA) and **company confidential** business information. The rules differ.

Dr Anith Chacko is Radhiant's Information Officer.

## Patient data: the hard lines

- Patient-level information lives only in the systems built for it: the Admin Portal and the clinical systems. Nowhere else.
- **Never** copy patient names, ID numbers, contact details, results or appointment details into email, WhatsApp, chat tools, AI assistants (including this one), task descriptions, meeting notes or file names.
- Aggregate numbers are fine: "141 appointments this week" is safe. A named patient's appointment is not.
- The recall programme runs under a written POPIA carve-out, with a lawful basis, data minimisation and an audit trail. Recall work happens inside that system only.
- If a patient asks for their data to be corrected or deleted, send the request to Sam (Admin).

## Company information

- Assume everything in this app and the Working Folders is **Internal** or **Confidential**. Do not share it outside Radhiant without approval.
- Financial figures, legal positions and regulatory claims carry a confidence tag when they are estimates. Never quote an estimate as a verified number.
- If you are unsure whether something can be shared, treat it as the higher confidentiality tier and ask.

## This app

Radhiant Brain holds business knowledge only. It contains no patient data by design, and the assistant will decline questions that ask for patient-level information. If you see anything in here that looks like personal or patient data, tell Anith straight away so it can be removed.

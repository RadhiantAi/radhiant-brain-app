## Daily operations

- **Radhiant Admin Portal** (portal-admin.rdisa.co.za): the operational hub. Appointments, patients, calendars, truck trip approval, reports, forms, tickets and the Company Dashboard used in the L10.
- **The Radhiant CRM** (crm.rdisa.co.za): our own in-house CRM, built and run by Damon's team. It holds the company pipeline. We do not use HubSpot or Zoho.
- **Microsoft 365**: email, calendars, Teams, Planner tasks and the SharePoint Working Folders.
- **INFINITT RIS and PACS**: reporting and the image archive. The desktop app and the web portal are two different things, so say which one you mean.
- **Xero**: invoicing. Every invoice goes through Xero.
- **3CX**: the office phones.
- **Plaud**: records and transcribes the L10.

## Patient-facing

- **HerWay** (radhiant.me): the monthly subscription.
- **Recall reminders** to past patients go out by WhatsApp from our own system, under the POPIA recall carve-out. Patient data stays there and in the portal.

## No longer used

HubSpot, Smartsheet and Papermark are closed.

## Radhiant Brain

This app. It explains approved processes and helps you prepare drafts. It does not read live bookings, completions or revenue, and it cannot change anything in another system.

## Who to ask

System access and accounts: **Damon** or **Vincent**. Documents in the Working Folders: your head of department.

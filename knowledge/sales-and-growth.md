We call this work **outreach**. We never call it sales.

## Channels

1. **Workplace screening**: screening days at employers.
2. **Medical aid members**: insured women booking their own screening.
3. **Community and event screening**: activations at sites across the provinces.
4. **Cash patients**: R1,200 for a screening mammogram.
5. **HerWay**: our monthly subscription (below).

## Who looks after what

- Corporate leads and partnerships sit with **Anith**, supported by **Jen Coetzee** (fractional CMO).
- **Tammy Gratz** looks after day-to-day outreach team matters and training.
- **Drivon** (Cath Royden-Turner) does lead generation and social media.
- The pipeline is kept in the **Radhiant CRM** (crm.rdisa.co.za). Jen signs off the pipeline.
- Past patients get the WhatsApp booking link. We do not phone them to rebook.

## HerWay (radhiant.me)

These terms were approved on 23 October 2026.

| Plan | Price | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | R89 a month | One adult woman |
| Family | R169.10 a month | Exactly two adult women |
| Give One | R159 a month | The sponsor plan. It replaced Sponsor a Sister. |

- The commitment is 12 months.
- The waiting period is three months on every plan. A member can book from month four, after three payments.
- There are no partner exceptions to the waiting period.
- The Premium plan has been withdrawn.
- A third person takes a Basic plan. Four people need two Family plans.
- The mammogram is "included". Never call it "free".

**Cancellation** (wording approved by Anith on 10 November 2026):

> A member may cancel at any time on 20 business days' written notice. Before the included mammogram has been done, payments made are refunded less an administration charge of R50. After the included mammogram has been done, the member pays the difference between the cash price of a screening mammogram (R1,200) and the subscription payments already made, up to the balance of the 12-month subscription.

Sam works out a member's cancellation amount. Do not work it out yourself.

## Mammogram Match

For every paid mammogram booked with RDISA, the Radhiant Foundation provides one free mammogram to a woman without medical aid. Mammogram Match is permanent. Do not quote old campaign end dates.

## Booking numbers

Whether a truck day goes ahead depends on confirmed patients against the minimum for that day: 10, 12 or 15 depending on distance. See the Fleet and daily operations entry.

The Radhiant Admin Portal at **portal-admin.rdisa.co.za** is the day-to-day operational system for RDISA. Sign-in is by email and password. Damon manages accounts under Settings, Staff.

## Menu

This is the portal menu as seen on Tuesday 10 November 2026. What you see depends on the access your account has been given.

| Menu | Items |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Overview; Company Dashboard (what the L10 runs on) |
| Patients | All Patients; Waiting List; No-shows; Doctors |
| Calendar | Calendar |
| Mammogram Trucks | Overview; Sites |
| CRM | Opens the Radhiant CRM, our own in-house system |
| Reports | Appointment Reports; Performance Report; NPS; Patient Reports; Site Reports |
| Settings | Staff; Duplicated patients; Email; Email Sync; SMS; Code generation; Logs; Scorecard; Planner; Fleet logistics |
| T&T | Opens Track and Trace |
| Forms | Document; Patient Forms; Surveys; Quotes |
| Tickets | Log a ticket |

The Patients menu and the patient reports hold POPIA-protected data. Handle them with care.

## House rules

- Patient information stays inside the portal. Never copy patient details into email, WhatsApp, chat tools or this app.
- Scorecard metrics are updated every week before the Monday L10 at 10:00.
- Managers file their weekly report in the portal on Friday.
- Site Owners update their sites on the portal by 16:00 every working day (Site Booking SOP).
- Access problems or bugs: log a ticket in the portal, or ask Damon.

Radhiant runs a lean structure: a founder and CEO, five department heads and the teams under them. People who have left Radhiant are not listed here.

## Leadership

- **Dr Anith Chacko**: Founder, Director and Chief Executive. A specialist radiologist. He is also Radhiant's Information Officer under POPIA.
- **Jen Coetzee**: fractional Chief Marketing Officer. Jen signs off outreach volume validation, the pipeline and marketing forecasts.

## The five department heads

| Name | Title | Looks after |
|---|---|---|
| Samantha le Roux | Head of Admin | Billing, corporate collections and administration |
| Fatima del Valle | Head of Clinical Operations | Clinical operations, QA and radiographer credentials |
| Irshad Haniff | Head of Logistics and Operations | Trucks, equipment, drivers and site bookings |
| Damon du Randt | Head of IT | The Admin Portal, the Radhiant CRM, devices and access |
| Tamryn (Tammy) Gratz | Outreach Ambassador | Day-to-day outreach team matters, sharing experience and training |

Notes:

- Fatima del Valle appears as Fatima Davids on some older documents. It is the same person.
- Tammy reports to Jen and Anith. The other Outreach Ambassadors report to Tammy and Jen. Her role does not carry financial, contractual or commercial approval.

## Support roles

- **Abby Geiling** is Anith's PA and schedules his external meetings.
- **Vincent Langley** is Deputy Head of IT.
- **Erryn-Larnelle Garnie** works in Operations and Logistics with Irshad.
- **Catherine (Cath) Royden-Turner** at Drivon is our outside agency for lead generation and social media.

- Clinical protocol, truck QA, radiographers: **Fatima**.
- Truck movement, equipment, sites: **Irshad**.
- Portal access, email, devices, systems: **Damon** or **Vincent**.
- Corporate leads and partnerships: **Anith**, supported by **Jen**.
- Outreach team matters: **Tammy**.
- Anith's diary: **Abby**.

## The numbers that decide a truck day

These come from the Site Booking SOP V1.7 of 14 October 2026. It is the working version and is not yet signed, so check the portal for a newer one.

Minimums are counted **per truck day**, not per site.

| Type of truck day | Minimum confirmed patients |
|---|---|
| Local site (50 km or less from base, same-day return) | 10 |
| Day trip (more than 50 km from base, same-day return) | 12 |
| Overnight trip, outbound day and return day | 15 each |
| Overnight trip, days in between | 10 or 12, by distance from the accommodation |

Distance is one-way road distance from the operating base. A site at exactly 50 km is Local.

Break-even is 8.7 patients per truck day, within 50 km on a normal working day, at R1,650 per scan excluding VAT (estimated; from the SOP).

## The countdown

- **T-8** (eight days before the visit): the Go or No-Go decision, recorded on the portal by 16:30. From here the Site Owner contacts the site champion every day.
- **T-7**: sites are told the decision by 16:00. Confirmed patients are told by the end of T-8, or by 12:00 on T-7 at the latest. A backup site is named by T-7.
- **T-3**: a message to the site, and a confirmation call to every booked patient.
- **T-1**: a message to the site, and the 16:00 read of booked names.

A truck day at or below its minimum at T-8 is worked to fill it first: a backup site, a split day, or a half day rebooked nearby. A day still below its minimum after that does not go ahead on its booked date, and the Site Owner reschedules it. Exceptions need the CEO's approval and are capped at three per unit per calendar month.

A missed portal update does not cancel a truck day that has its numbers.

## Quality and compliance

Mammography follows the RDISA Imaging SOP V2.0 (29 May 2026), which covers positioning, compression QA, reject analysis and image transmission. Equipment is licensed under SAHPRA. Fatima's team tracks radiographer credentials.

## Recall

Past patients who are due for a follow-up or annual mammogram are reminded under a written POPIA carve-out policy. Patient-level recall data stays inside the systems built for it.

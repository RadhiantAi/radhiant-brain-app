From the RDISA Vision, mission and values document, Version 4.4 of 28 September 2026. These commitments apply to everyone at RDISA, including leadership, operations and patient care.

## Our eight values

1. **Dignity first.** We treat every woman with warmth and respect. We protect her privacy, listen without judgement and give her the time she needs.
2. **Compassion with impact.** We act with care and follow through when someone needs help.
3. **Trust is sacred.** We earn trust by being honest, consistent and keeping our word to patients, partners and each other. We check every number we state about ourselves against a source before using it.
4. **Excellence without exception.** Clinical accuracy, operational reliability and professional conduct are required in every part of our work.
5. **Courage together.** We speak honestly and take responsibility. We address problems early and respectfully. Anyone who sees a clinical, privacy, consent, capacity or workload problem in their own area may stop that work.
6. **One team with one standard.** The same standards of behaviour apply to every role and at every level, including when we are under pressure.
7. **Innovation that serves.** We adopt technology and new ideas only when they improve access, quality or women's experience of care. A person stays accountable for every clinical decision.
8. **No woman left behind.** We work to remove barriers caused by distance, cost, lack of information and fear.

Each value maps to the CASTLE principles (Courage, Authenticity, Service, Truth, Love, Effectiveness). CASTLE and the phrase "no woman left behind" are for use inside RDISA only. Do not use them in external, recruitment or marketing material.

## The limits that come before targets

These seven limits apply to every target and measure:

1. Care must be clinically appropriate.
2. People must retain authority over decisions.
3. There must be enough capacity to provide the next steps in care safely.
4. We must protect privacy.
5. We must respect consent.
6. Access must be fair.
7. Workloads must be sustainable.

A gain does not count if it breaks any of these limits. We adjust any booking target that conflicts with them.

## Using the values

The values guide hiring and promotion, leadership behaviour and performance discussions. Use them when choosing partners or deciding what takes priority when demands conflict. A decision that contradicts them should not proceed, whatever the short-term gain.
